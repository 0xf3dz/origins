Origins

Born in Naples, raised in Luxembourg and studied in Rotterdam. I graduated with a BSc with a double major in Psychology and Law from Erasmus University College, where I wrote my thesis on judicial biases. After university, I chose an unconventional path, learning through experience—working in diverse environments, adapting under pressure, and building a broad skill set.

My journey took me across the globe, starting as a scuba-diving instructor in Indonesia, then joining marine conservation NGO Sea Shepherd to cross the North Atlantic ocean by boat from Iceland to Canada, and later taking part in operation Milagro in Mexico, patrolling the Sea of Cortez against the Mexican cartel. Shortly after, I worked as a tree planter in Queensland, Australia as part of a revegetation project following the wildfires. These gritty land and oceanic adventures incredibly shaped my work ethic by not only giving me a lifetime of memories and stories to tell but also by building resilience and the ability to collaborate under extreme pressure.

In a period of financial need, I discovered crypto markets—a space rife with inefficiencies and volatility that offered opportunities for those willing to act fast and strategically. I recognized the potential in leveraging publicly available information and over time, optimized my edge by running faster executions with AWS servers, building a curated wallet-tracking database, and doing discretionary blockchain research. Trading full-time for three years, I grew my portfolio by 200x, learning to combine data-driven analysis with disciplined risk management, all while maintaining agency in a highly competitive market.

My trading success enabled me to purchase my first sailboat, fully financed by my profits. In parallel to my trading career, I worked weekends as a boat captain, living aboard a self-sufficient sailboat. This balance of trading and sailing further reinforced my resilience and autonomy.

Eventually, I hit a professional and personal plateau, which led me to take a step back: I moved to Lisbon to pursue a MSc in Business Analytics, crystallising my trading experience and refining my technical skills.

In my free time, I like to progress in my hobbies, surfing, sailing and freediving; drink good specialty coffee or read sci-fi novels. I also like to play chess and poker.
